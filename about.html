About us

Care that starts with looking, not selling

Bellure Clinic opened because four of us kept hearing the same complaint: cosmetology that feels like a sales funnel. So we built the opposite — a small clinic where the doctor’s opinion comes before any product.

How a visit works

The same four steps, every time.

  1. Consultation

    A doctor examines your skin and asks about your goals and health history.

  2. A written plan

    You get the recommended treatment, expected results, cost and downtime — in writing.

  3. Treatment

    Certified products and single-use tools only, performed by the doctor who examined you.

  4. Aftercare

    Clear aftercare notes and a follow-up check, included in the price.

What we value

Honesty over upselling

If a treatment won’t help, we say so — even if it costs us the sale.

Documented safety

Every product is registered and every tool is single-use or sterilized on-site.

Realistic expectations

We describe results in ranges, not guarantees — skin responds differently for everyone.
